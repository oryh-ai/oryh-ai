# ORYH

ORYH is an AI-native OA/BPM platform. The business logic does not live in the
server — it lives in the Skills an agent loads on behalf of a person, and the
server records what actually happened.

## Start here

- **[AI-native OA/BPM positioning](platform/ai-native-platform.md)** — what the
  platform is for, and what it deliberately does not do.
- **[Capabilities, Skills and the API](platform/capabilities-skills-api.md)** —
  how a role's capabilities become the Skills a person's agent is allowed to run.
- **[Agent self-connect](platform/device-flow.md)** — how an agent obtains a
  device credential without ever seeing your password.

## Business objects

Each document explains one object and the reasoning behind its shape — why the
absence is the record, why a payslip is an invoice, why one table serves both
retail and B2B.
